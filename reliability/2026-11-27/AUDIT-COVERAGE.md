### N5 measurements and visual inspection

- The native C/C++ profile observed **zero allocations/frees over 2,188 callbacks**, including cold consumption, SysEx, dense input releases, replacement, bypass and panic. Its host MIDI buffer was reserved to **4 MiB**. Mixed-size callback mean was 109.945 µs and maximum 3,135 µs; those timings are diagnostic, not a common block-budget threshold.
- The short virtual-CoreMIDI stall measured 2.08995 seconds and 17 voice-1 onsets. Steady-period drift was −2.10979 ms, excluding the one immediate initial onset; 16 grid-to-grid intervals were measured. Approximate delivery lateness p50/p95/p99 was 3.0/7.4/7.7 ms, maximum 7.677 ms. It recorded zero output failures and overruns, balanced final note identities, and 83 dropped visual updates during the stall. This is not the unexecuted 30-minute soak.
- Metal completed **27 actual drawable frames**, uploaded **13,824 glyph bytes**, handled **all 21 injected allocation failures**, and reported **zero GPU errors**, with Metal validation enabled. It exercised short resize/hide/show and readback paths.
- UI passed all **15 page/size layouts**, modes/units, keyboard and presentation behavior, strict/exact numeric tests and dialog-owner races. Five corrected advanced-dialog captures were visually inspected: leading signs/digits and ASCII range labels are now readable. The actual tooltip-text and leading-character assertions passed; no separate hover screenshot was taken. Earlier main-page captures had also been inspected. This does not cover Retina/multiple display scales or complete DAW interaction.

1. Distinguish implemented repair, passed regression, native candidate pass and real-host/device/display qualification. Do not label all A01–A16 or W0–W8 closed.
2. Preserve branch-head and tested-merge identities. N5's **18/18 integration pass** and **later AU failure** are both true; subsequent candidate changes require their own evidence.
3. Retain the host-owned MIDI-buffer and final host-teardown limitations. Their external ownership cannot be eliminated by a core event-sink test.
4. Keep simulated clock, short real virtual-CoreMIDI stall, future 30-minute virtual soak, physical MIDI and combined UI/GPU stress results distinct.
5. Distinguish historical user sessions, synthetic migrations and actual prior-executable generated fixtures. Likewise distinguish step-refinement traces from before/after-repair compatibility traces.
6. M5 supplies the previously missing per-preset musical refinement evidence without changing the model. Its observed sensitivity is not a reason by itself to alter the solver, presets or thresholds.
