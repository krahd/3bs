# The Three Body Solution

## Goal

The Three Body Solution (3bs) is an MIDI Instrument Plugin (VST3/AU) and standalone that generates MIDI using a three-body simulation. The user defines the masses of the three bodies, the gravitational constant (either choosing from pre-defined values, setting random values, changing existing values with the siders or entering them manually) and then lets the simulation run at the chosen speed (which can be also changed mid-execution). 

3bs renders in 3D the simulation, gorgeously. The camera can be centred on any of the bodies. The camera can also orbit the selected body. The bodies draw an orbit on the 3D space that fades after a little while (in the settings the graphical parameters can be manipulated, turned on and off, etc.).

The centred body can also move forward in 3D space, so that the trails of the bodies are in 3D, in a kind of spiral pattern (such as if the whole system were oribiting around a sun).

Metaphorically, the three bodies are thought of as planets, or a planet and two moons. The 3D drawing reinforces that by rendering them as if they were planets, with a sun far away that lights them, stars in the background and planet textures and normal maps on them.

The movement of the planets (that is, the bodies) can be mapped to musical scales, so that the whole instrument acts as an improvising system. There are different modes of mapping and different scales to map them to (pentatonic, blues, chromatic, lucian, phrigian, hungarian, etc. and, each planet can have a different root note, if so chosen by the user).

The mapping could be the distance from the bodies to each other, distance to a plane, etc. We should investigate the most intuitive modes of mapping.
